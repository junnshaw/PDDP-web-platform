Using Transphos Requires Only 3 Steps




Step 1: Select the site you want to predict

You can select 'S','T' or 'Y' . Representing serine , threonine and tyrosine respectively . This is the amino acid you want to predict if phosphorylation is occurring . By default, 'S' will be selected .


Step 2: Input the data

You can enter multiple data with a carriage return . Please follow the following format for data entry .
Format: Protein, Site position, Protein sequence
